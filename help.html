Emergency Help

📞 Cyber Crime Helpline

Helpline Number: 1930

If you become a victim of online fraud, immediately call 1930.

🌐 Report Cyber Crime

You can report cyber crimes on the official Government of India cyber crime portal.

🛡️ Emergency Tips

  • Block the scammer immediately.
  • Do not share OTP, Password or UPI PIN.
  • Inform your bank if money is deducted.
  • Report the incident as soon as possible.
  • Keep screenshots as evidence.
