🔐 Two-Factor Authentication

Learn how 2FA helps protect your online accounts.

🛡️ What is 2FA?

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is an extra security step used when logging into an account.

It requires two different forms of verification instead of relying only on a password.

❓ Why should you use 2FA?

  • 🔐 Protects your account if your password is stolen.
  • 🛡️ Adds an extra layer of security.
  • 🚫 Helps prevent unauthorized logins.
  • 🔑 Makes your important accounts safer.

⚙️ How to use 2FA?

  1. Open your account's Security settings.
  2. Find the Two-Factor Authentication option.
  3. Enable 2FA and choose an available method.
  4. Complete the setup and save your recovery information safely.
  5. The next time you log in, complete the second verification step.

📱 Common 2FA Methods

  • OTP: A temporary verification code.
  • Authenticator App: Generates temporary verification codes.
  • Security Key: A physical device used for account verification.

⚠️ 2FA Safety Tips

  • Never share your OTP or verification code.
  • Never approve an unexpected login request.
  • Use 2FA on important accounts whenever available.
  • Keep your recovery codes private and secure.
