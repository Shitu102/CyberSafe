ℹ️ About This App

Cyber Security Awareness App

This application is designed to help users identify cyber scams and improve their online safety awareness.

It allows users to check suspicious SMS and emails, learn cyber safety tips, test their knowledge through a quiz, and access emergency cyber crime support.

✨ Main Features

  • 📩 Check SMS Messages
  • 📧 Check Emails
  • ⚠️ Scam Awareness
  • 🧠 Cyber Quiz
  • 🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips
  • 🚨 Emergency Help

🎯 Project Objective

To spread cyber security awareness and help people stay safe from online frauds, phishing attacks, fake messages and cyber crimes.
